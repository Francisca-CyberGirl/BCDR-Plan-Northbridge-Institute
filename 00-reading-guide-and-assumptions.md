# Reading guide and assumptions

## How to read this report


The six tasks follow in order, with calculations and matrices in tables and the reasoning in the text. The Task 5 schedule gives **modelled baseline results**, not a live test, and the tabletop is a **simulation**. All data are fictional. Week 21 slide citations refer to the instructor’s teaching deck (ICDFA, n.d.). Table 1 lists my assumptions (A1–A12).
